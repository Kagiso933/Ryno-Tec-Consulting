## Revenue Model

### Service Revenue Streams

1. **Project-Based (40% of revenue)**
   - Fixed-price projects (infrastructure builds, migrations)
   - Time & materials (advisory, optimization)
   - Hybrid models
   - Average project: R300K - R2M
   - Duration: 1-6 months

2. **Managed Services (30% of revenue)**
   - Recurring monthly support and monitoring
   - SLA-based support tiers
   - Optimization and performance management
   - Average: R20K - R100K per month per client
   - Sticky revenue (multi-year contracts)

3. **Advisory & Strategy (20% of revenue)**
   - Hourly consulting (R1500-R3000/hour depending on complexity)
   - Roadmapping and planning (fixed-price engagements)
   - Assessment services
   - Strategic guidance

4. **Training & Knowledge Transfer (10% of revenue)**
   - Technical training programs
   - Certification preparation
   - Internal capability building
   - Workshops and seminars
