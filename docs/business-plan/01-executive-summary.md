## 🏢 Business Concept

Axiom Infrastructure bridges a critical gap in the South African market: the shortage of specialised expertise in advanced platform engineering, Azure modern workplace management, and AI infrastructure. Unlike traditional IT consulting that offers broad generalist services, Axiom focuses deeply on these three areas where enterprise demand significantly exceeds available supply.

### The Market Opportunity

- **Large enterprises** (1000+ employees) increasingly require sophisticated infrastructure and platform engineering capabilities
- **Financial services sector** (SA's largest IT spend) needs expertise in compliance-heavy environments
- **Government and public sector** organisations need specialised digital transformation support
- **High-growth startups** require cloud-native infrastructure and MLOps capabilities
- **Skills shortage** in these specialised areas creates premium pricing and high demand

## 💼 Service Offerings

### Three Core Service Lines

1. **Platform Engineering Consulting** (40% revenue target)
   - Infrastructure as Code (Bicep, ARM, Terraform)
   - DevOps and CI/CD transformation
   - Cloud-native architecture design
   - Automation and scripting services
   - **Average Project Value:** R750k - R2.5M

2. **Azure Modern Workplace Management** (35% revenue target)
   - AVD design and optimization
   - Entra ID and identity governance
   - Intune device management
   - Microsoft 365 deployments and optimization
   - **Average Engagement:** R100k - R500k project + R25k-100k/month retainer

3. **AI Infrastructure & MLOps** (25% revenue target)
   - MLOps platform architecture
   - AI/ML pipeline design and implementation
   - Model deployment and monitoring
   - Enterprise AI governance
   - **Average Project Value:** R500k - R2M

### Service Model

- **Project-based work** - Fixed-scope consulting projects (60% revenue)
- **Retainer services** - Ongoing strategic guidance and support (40% revenue)
- **Hybrid pricing** - Fixed + variable pricing based on outcomes
