## 🎯 Service Focus

### Three Core Pillars

1. **Platform Engineering** (40% of revenue)
   - Infrastructure as Code (Bicep, Terraform)
   - DevOps and CI/CD
   - Cloud-native architecture
   - Automation and scripting

2. **Azure Modern Workplace** (35% of revenue)
   - AVD management and optimization
   - Entra ID and identity governance
   - Intune device management
   - Microsoft 365 deployments

3. **AI Infrastructure** (25% of revenue)
   - MLOps platform design
   - AI/ML pipeline architecture
   - Model deployment and monitoring
   - Enterprise AI governance

## 🎯 Target Market

### Primary Segments

- **Large Enterprises** (1000+ employees)
  - Digital transformation initiatives
  - Platform engineering needs
  - Compliance-heavy (POPIA)
  - Examples: Capitec, Nedbank, Discovery, Sasol

- **Financial Services**
  - High compliance requirements
  - Mission-critical systems
  - Security-focused
  - Infrastructure modernization

- **Government & Public Sector**
  - B-BBEE compliance
  - Large-scale deployments
  - Regulatory requirements

- **Tech Startups & Scale-ups**
  - Cloud-native infrastructure
  - Rapid scaling needs
  - AI/ML capability building

### Secondary Segments

- Professional services firms
- Logistics and supply chain
- Healthcare organizations
- Retail and e-commerce
