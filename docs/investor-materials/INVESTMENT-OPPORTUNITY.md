## ⚠️ Risk Analysis & Mitigation

### Key Risks

| Risk | Impact | Probability | Mitigation |
|------|--------|-----------|-----------|
| **Slower Customer Acquisition** | Revenue delays, extended burn | Medium | Strong network, warm outreach, referrals |
| **Execution Risk** | Project delays, quality issues | Low | Conservative scoping, process discipline |
| **Market Competition** | Price pressure, lost deals | Medium | Deep specialization, differentiation |
| **Founder Burnout** | Key person risk, quality issues | Low | Sustainable pace, early hiring, processes |
| **Market Downturn** | Reduced IT budgets | Medium | Focus on ROI-positive services, cost savings |
| **Client Concentration** | Revenue volatility | Medium | Diversify client base, mix of deal sizes |

### Mitigation Strategies

1. **Customer Acquisition Risk**
   - Strong professional network and relationships
   - Multiple customer acquisition channels
   - Partnership model for inbound leads
   - Reputation built through case studies

2. **Delivery Risk**
   - Conservative project scoping
   - Well-defined service delivery processes
   - Contractor/freelance support for workload
   - Early hiring of delivery specialists

3. **Market Risk**
   - Focus on essential, ROI-positive services
   - Retainer model provides revenue stability
   - Diversified service lines reduce dependency
   - Long sales cycles = revenue pipeline visibility

4. **Team Risk**
   - Clear business processes reduce key person dependency
   - Planned hiring in Year 2 scales capability
   - Documented methodologies and templates
   - Partnership and contractor network
